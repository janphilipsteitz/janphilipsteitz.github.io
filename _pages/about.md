Welcome to my website. I am a fourth-year PhD student in Economics at the
University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee, and I am on the 2026–2027 job market.

My research interests are in public economics, economic history, and labor
economics. I am particularly interested in state and local taxation, fiscal
federalism, interjurisdictional tax competition, the historical development of
the American tax system, and immigration.

[here]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/).
